What can a universal charger really charge?

The clips are for compatible removable batteries with matching contacts and voltage. The two battery sizes here are imagined compatible cells. “Universal” does not mean every object fits.

The earphone in your reference is wired: it has no charging battery. Rechargeable USB devices need a supported USB output and cable, not these clamps; this object's USB capability is unverified.

Compare Hama's universal charger manual and Apple's wired-earphone guide. These describe other products, not the exact charger shown here.
